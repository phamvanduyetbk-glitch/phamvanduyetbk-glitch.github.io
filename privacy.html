Privacy Policy · Chính sách bảo mật

Effective date: 1 November 2026

1. Who we are

ERP Viet An ("the application") is an internal production management web application operated by Viet An Joint Stock Company ("the company"). It is used only by company employees. It is not offered to the public.

2. What data the application uses

  • Company operating data entered by employees: production output, working hours, quality defects, production plans, attendance, leave requests, gate passes and approvals.
  • Employee information needed for this work: employee number, name, line, factory and role.
  • Google user data: the application uses the Google Sheets and Google Drive permissions of the company's own Google account only to read, write and back up the company's own spreadsheets used by this application. It does not read your Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other personal Google data.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to run the company's internal production management: entering reports, calculating productivity, producing reports and Excel files, and storing monthly backups. The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties, except where the law requires it.

4. Where the data is stored

The data is stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive files owned by the company's Google account, and in backup Excel files on company computers and the company's OneDrive. Access is limited to company employees according to their role.

5. Retention and deletion

The company keeps operating data as long as it is needed for production management and legal record keeping. An employee may ask the company to correct or delete their personal information by contacting the address below.

6. Security

Each employee signs in with their own account and password. Passwords are stored as hashes. Each role can only see and change the data of its own lines and factories.

7. Use of Google API data

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
